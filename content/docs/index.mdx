Threadbase streams Claude Code and Codex sessions from your own machines to your phone.
You can approve, answer, queue the next prompt, or start a fresh session from anywhere.

## How it works

Threadbase has two parts:

- **The streamer** (`tb-streamer`) runs on each machine where your agents run — macOS, Linux, or Windows.
  It starts and manages agent sessions and serves them to your phone.
- **The app** runs on iOS and Android and connects to the streamers you pair it with — directly by default.

<Callout title="Your sessions stay on your machines">
  The app talks to streamers you run, and by default nothing sits in between.
  An optional relay, off by default, can carry that traffic if you would rather not set up remote access yourself.
  It only ever sees encrypted bytes and connection metadata, never your sessions.
  Your sessions and history stay on your machines.
</Callout>
