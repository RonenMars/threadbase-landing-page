Threadbase streams Claude Code and Codex sessions from your own machines to your phone.
You can approve, answer, queue the next prompt, or start a fresh session from anywhere.

## How it works

Threadbase has two parts:

- **The streamer** (`tb-streamer`) runs on each machine where your agents run — macOS, Linux, or Windows.
  It starts and manages agent sessions and serves them to your phone.
- **The app** runs on iOS and Android and connects directly to the streamers you pair it with.

<Callout title="There is no hosted service">
  The app talks only to streamers you run.
  Your sessions and history stay on your machines.
</Callout>
